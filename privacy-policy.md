# Privacy Policy for MotionSound: Custom Soundboard

**Effective date:** September 6, 2026

MotionSound: Custom Soundboard is provided by **Idanprog**.
For privacy questions, contact **idanprog@gmail.com**.

## Data the app handles

When you select a custom sound, the app reads the selected audio file and its
filename. It stores the sound in private app storage on your Android phone and
copies it, together with its name and soundboard settings, to the Wear OS watch
you pair with the app. A random per-installation identifier is used only to
coordinate a library restore between your paired devices.

The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your audio
files, filenames, soundboard settings, or installation identifier. The app does
not include advertising or developer analytics. Android backup is limited to the
anonymous sync marker needed to restore upload-history protection after a
reinstall; it does not include audio files, sound library records, or the
purchase-entitlement cache.

Google Play processes purchases for the optional unlimited-uploads product.
Google's handling of purchase information is governed by Google's
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Watch motion sensors are used locally to detect gestures that trigger sounds.
Sensor readings are not sent to the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Custom sounds remain in the app's private storage until you delete them. Deleting
a custom sound removes it from the phone and queues its removal from the paired
watch. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes local app
data. You can also remove the app from the paired watch.

## Sharing

The app transfers selected audio and related soundboard data only to your paired
Wear OS device to provide the soundboard feature. It does not sell personal data
or share it for advertising.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current
version is available at https://idanl08.github.io/motionsound-privacy/privacy-policy.html.
