# Privacy Policy for MotionSound: Custom Soundboard

**Effective date:** September 6, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 7, 2026

## Who is responsible and how to contact us

MotionSound: Custom Soundboard is provided by **Idan Levy**, operating as
**Idanprog** from **Israel**. This policy covers the Android phone app, Wear OS app,
support communications, and this policy website.

For privacy questions or requests, email **idanprog@gmail.com**. For information
we receive and decide how to use, such as support correspondence, the app
operator is responsible for that processing.

## Information used by the app

**Your sound library.** When you choose an audio file through Android's file
picker, the app reads that file and its filename and stores a copy in private
app storage. Sound names, favorites, ordering, gesture bindings, and related
library records are stored to provide the features you use. Files may contain
personal information, including voices; choose carefully what you import.
The app does not record your microphone or require a separate MotionSound account.

**Phone and watch synchronization.** The app uses Google's Wearable Data Layer
to exchange selected audio, library settings, deletion records, and synchronization
information between your paired devices. Transfers and deletion messages may be
queued until a device reconnects. We do not operate a server that receives your
library through this synchronization process.

**Installation and upload history.** A random installation identifier, restore
status, and lifetime custom-upload count help synchronize the devices, detect
reinstallation, restore library state, and enforce the free upload allowance.
This identifier is not your name or advertising ID, but we do not describe it as
fully anonymous. Deleting a sound does not reset its upload-history count.

**Motion sensors.** The watch processes accelerometer and, where available,
gyroscope readings locally to recognize gestures and trigger playback. The app
does not transmit raw motion readings to the developer.

**Purchases.** Google Play processes payments. The app receives purchase status
and purchase tokens through Play Billing to verify and acknowledge purchases and
restore paid features. It caches the unlock status locally. The app does not
receive your full payment-card details. Google may make transaction information
available to the developer through its merchant and Play services.

**Diagnostics.** The app writes technical messages to Android's local system
logs, which may include file references, synchronization details, and errors.
There is no developer-operated automatic log-upload service in the app. Logs or
screenshots you choose to send for support are handled as support information.

The app does not include advertising or developer analytics and does not sell
your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

## Optional information and support

Importing custom sounds, purchasing an upgrade, and contacting support are your
choices. You can use bundled sounds without importing your own. Without a selected
file, the app cannot add that custom sound; without the relevant purchase, paid
features remain unavailable. If you contact support, we receive your email
address, message, and any attachments you send and use them to respond, investigate
the issue, and handle related requests. Do not send passwords, full payment-card
details, or unnecessary sensitive information. Email delivery and storage use Gmail.

## Other services and international processing

Google provides Play Billing, device services, and Gmail. Android backup follows

browser information, and request logs. The policy page has no analytics or
advertising scripts added by us.

These providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy
notices: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and

Support information may be accessed in Israel, and service providers may process
information in other countries. Protections and legal requirements can differ
between countries. Contact us for information about arrangements relevant to your
request. This policy does not replace any safeguards required by applicable law.
Information may also be disclosed where required by law; that does not give us
remote access to a library stored only on your devices.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

**Device data.** Your library remains in app storage until removed. Individual
sound deletion inside the current app requires MotionSound Pro. When available,
it removes the phone copy and attempts to notify the watch; reconnect the devices
and confirm removal. If a transfer fails, the watch copy can remain. Minimal
deletion records can remain to prevent deleted sounds from returning during sync.

**Free removal of all local data.** You can clear app storage or uninstall the app
on both the phone and watch without purchasing Pro. Clearing only one device does
not clear the other; remaining paired-device data can be restored during later
synchronization. App deletion does not remove the original audio file from the
location where you selected it, or cancel/refund a Google Play purchase.

**Backup and reinstall.** The phone permits Android to back up only sync identity
preferences, including a prior installation identifier and restore flags. Audio,
library databases, and the purchase-unlock cache are excluded from that backup.
The watch disables Android app backup. A retained phone backup or paired watch
can preserve restore/upload-limit state after reinstalling. Backup retention and
removal are controlled through the relevant device or backup provider; uninstalling
does not promise deletion of provider-held backups or Google's purchase records.

**Support and transaction records.** We retain information we receive only for
as long as needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support history,
handle disputes, or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. The purpose,
status of a dispute, and applicable legal period determine retention. You can
request deletion of correspondence; where a legal reason prevents deletion, we
will explain the applicable limitation. Provider-held records follow the
provider's retention arrangements.

## Security

Library files are kept in Android private app storage and access is restricted by
the operating system. Paired-device communication uses Google's Wearable services;
this policy website uses HTTPS. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee
that a device, email account, or service can never be compromised. Keep your
devices updated and protected with a screen lock. We cannot remotely retrieve or
erase a library that exists only on your devices.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access or correct
personal information, request deletion or restriction, object to processing,
receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
Email **idanprog@gmail.com** to make a request. We may need proportionate
information to verify the request and will respond within applicable legal time
limits. We will explain when we do not hold the requested information or when a
legal exception applies. Requests concerning Google's or GitHub's independently

Where EEA or UK data-protection law applies, processing necessary to provide
requested app or purchase functionality is based on performance of the service
contract; responding to support and preventing abuse may rely on legitimate
interests, subject to your rights; legally required recordkeeping relies on legal
obligations. Consent is requested where required for optional processing and can
be withdrawn for future processing. This notice is not blanket consent to new uses.

You can complain to the relevant privacy authority, including the Israeli Privacy
Protection Authority or the authority in your country where applicable. Nothing
in this policy waives your statutory rights or limits remedies provided by law.

## Younger users

MotionSound is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to
children under 13. Where local law requires parental authorization for a minor's
use or a particular processing activity, that requirement still applies.
We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13 through
support. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information
to us can contact **idanprog@gmail.com** to request review and deletion, subject
to applicable law. For sound data held only on a child's devices, use the local
deletion steps above; we cannot remotely access those files.
